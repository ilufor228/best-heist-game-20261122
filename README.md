An original top-down stealth heist game built for the `best-heist-game-20261122-0559` competition on [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/best-heist-game-20261122-0559).

## Gameplay
- **Infiltrate**: Sneak past guards with sweeping vision cones and rotating security cameras.
- **Loot**: Grab high-value loot bags scattered throughout the bank.
- **Crack**: Crack the secure vault to steal the grand prize.
- **Escape**: Reach the extraction zone before the alarm timer runs out or detection triggers the full lockdown!
- **Audio**: Immersive sound effects powered entirely by WebAudio.
- **Multiple Levels**: Progressive difficulty across 3 intricate bank floor plans.

## Controls
- **Movement**: `WASD` or `Arrow Keys`
- **Crack Vault**: Stand near Vault and press `Space`
- **Start / Restart**: Click or press `Space` / `R`
